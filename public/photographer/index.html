About

I make quiet, deliberate portraits — the kind that hold a person still long enough to feel like them.

Elise Moreau is an editorial and portrait photographer working between Paris and London. Her work has appeared across independent fashion titles, brand campaigns and private commissions, always built around natural light and a restrained, film-led palette.

She shoots on a mix of medium-format film and digital, printing every series by hand. Whether it's a cover story or a single portrait, the approach is the same: time, trust, and a great deal of looking.
