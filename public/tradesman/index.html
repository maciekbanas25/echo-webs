Our story

We treat your garden like it's our own.

Maple & Moss started in 2012 with one van and a simple idea: show up when we say, do honest work, and leave every garden tidier than we found it. A decade on, that has not changed.

From a one-off tidy-up to a full redesign, you get the same crew, clear pricing, and someone who actually picks up the phone.

  • The same friendly crew on every visit
  • Free, no-pressure quotes within 24 hours
  • All green waste cleared and taken away
  • Insured, vetted team and a tidy finish
